SpinBreak
=========

A reversed pomodoro for your Mac: a few minutes of focused work, then a real break,
picked for you by a spin. Walk, read, clean a room, call someone. No more deciding.

INSTALL
1. Drag SpinBreak into Applications.
2. Open it. If macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't verify the developer":
   open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
   next to SpinBreak, and confirm. You only do this once.
3. Allow Calendar and Reminders when it asks:
   - Reminders: your break-over alarm, which also reaches your iPhone and Apple Watch.
   - Calendar: breaks end early before your meetings.

FIRST RUN: YOUR OWN AGENT SETS IT UP (about 10 minutes)
SpinBreak opens on one button.
1. Click "Copy setup prompt" and paste it into your agent (Claude Code, Codex,
   Cursor, or whatever you use).
2. Answer its questions. It builds your list and morning checklist, sets up your
   daily brief, and writes today's as a test.
3. Back in SpinBreak, click "Let's go".
Only have a chat AI in a browser? Paste the prompt there, copy its whole answer,
and click "Paste a chat AI's answer" under the button.
Prefer to do it by hand? The Activities, Balance, Schedule and Morning tabs edit
everything directly. Settings are always in the menu bar menu or Cmd-comma.

YOUR MORNING
Every workday opens full-screen on your morning checklist, next to your brief.
- Check everything off to start work.
- Need the laptop first? "I need the laptop for 5 minutes" steps the screen
  aside and brings it back, with your ticks kept.
- Skipped it? Your list and brief stay on the SpinBreak window (the Dock icon)
  all day, weekends included, next to how your day is going.

YOUR MORNING BRIEF
- If your AI set up the daily brief, SpinBreak runs it each morning after 5 am,
  as soon as your Mac is awake. Keep SpinBreak open (Settings › Schedule ›
  "Open SpinBreak at login"). Words of the day, phrases and quotes from past
  briefs are remembered, so they don't repeat. If a morning's brief fails,
  SpinBreak tries again, and Settings › Your AI shows what went wrong.
- With a chat AI: on the morning screen, "Copy today's request" → paste it into
  your AI → copy its answer → "Paste brief". "Step away 3 min" gets you out to
  do that.
- To change what's in it or how it sounds: Settings › Your AI › "How your brief
  is written".
The brief is made from today's calendar and yesterday's break notes, so your AI
can be specific. They only go to the AI you chose.

SHARING THE CLEANING WITH SOMEONE AT HOME (optional)
If you live with another SpinBreak user, Settings › Household explains it: one of
you shares a "SpinBreak Home" calendar with the other, and your spinners split the
rooms. Nobody gets the kitchen the other is already cleaning.

HOW A DAY GOES
- Press Go. Work until the timer runs out.
- The screen goes full-screen and spins up your break. Go do it.
  (Not right? Reroll, you get two. Room already clean? Tap "Already done".)
- When the break ends, write one line about how it went and start the next block.
- After your workday ends, the screen locks to "work's done". Hold the button for
  3 seconds if you really need 10 more minutes.
- Day off, or work's over, and you just want to be told what to do? "Give me a 25"
  spins up a break without starting work.

GETTING OUT
- The morning, break, day-off and evening screens have a "hold 3s" button to get you out.
  The spin has "1 more minute"; the end of a break asks for one line and lets you in.
- Quit from the menu bar tomato or the dock icon.
